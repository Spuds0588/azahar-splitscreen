Shared audio: player 1's console, measured
==========================================

Policy note: this file records the player-1-only policy of 2026-11-15. The owner
changed it on 2026-11-16 to an effects-tilted mix (every seat on the one output, seats
2-4 high-passed and trimmed); see `effects-tilt.txt`.

Policy (owner's decision, 2026-11-15): the session's single audio output is player 1's
console. Seats 2-4 stay on the null sink. There is no music/effects split, because there
is nothing to split: Azahar's HLE DSP mixes a console's music and effects into one stereo
`FinalMixSamples` stream before any sink (`src/audio_core/hle/`), and the four seats are
four separate processes. The app can choose which console's stream reaches the device; it
cannot take music from one console and effects from another.

That is what `src-tauri/src/slots.rs` already writes per seat (commit bb893d5f9):

    p1: output_type=@Variant(\0\0\0\x84\0\0\0\0\0\0\0\0)  volume=1      # Auto (0)
    p2: output_type=@Variant(\0\0\0\x84\0\0\0\0\0\0\0\x1)  volume=0      # Null (1)
    p3, p4: same as p2

This pass changed no code. It proves that policy against the actual device output and
records what a measurement of "the app's audio" has to look like.

How the capture was validated first
-----------------------------------
`pw-record --target alsa_output.pci-0000_00_1f.3.analog-stereo.monitor` does not error when
the name does not resolve: it silently records the default *source*, the microphone. Every
early capture in this pass was therefore the room mic, and it looked like a broken emulator:

    default-source capture: 2.94 s RMS -1.0 dBFS peak 32768   (a flat noise wall)
    spectrogram: broadband, no notes, no gaps

The same 330 Hz tone through the two paths:

    name-targeted capture (mic fallback): 330 Hz not measurable above the noise
    sink-id capture (--target 54):        0.0265 peak amplitude, -31.5 dBFS

With `pw-link -l` checked *during* the recording, the sink-id capture is the sink:

    alsa_output...:monitor_FL  |-> pw-record:input_FL
    alsa_output...:monitor_FR  |-> pw-record:input_FR

Everything below was recorded on that link. The sink's volume was set to 1.0 for the
measurements (it was 0.40 and muted before), then restored to 0.40 / muted.

One stream is the only thing feeding the device, and the 1-, 2- and 4-seat captures sit
within 1 dB of each other: if another seat's audio were being mixed in, the level would rise
by about +3 dB (uncorrelated) or +6 dB (same music), and there would be a second node.

No-leak control (the same seat, with its audio lines removed)
------------------------------------------------------------
`slots/p2/user` copied to /tmp/p2-audio, with only `[Audio]`'s `output_type` and `volume`
keys deleted, then launched as a standalone console on the same ROM:

    before: 1 stream (p1)                     capture -14.5 dBFS
    after:  2 streams (p1 + pid 1375064)      capture -10.2 dBFS, peak 32768

So the capture does see a second console, seat 2's console does render audio when it has a
sink, and what keeps it out of the session's output is the null sink - not a dead audio path.

Single-seat behaviour
---------------------
`players.clamp(2, 4)` means a one-player request still runs two seats (the layout needs two
tiles). Seat 1 opens the device, seat 2 does not, and the capture is the same as the two-seat
one within 1 dB, so a session that used to be "one console audible" still is.

Not done, on purpose
--------------------
- The music/effects split (player 1's music, everyone's effects) needs per-console access to
  the DSP's sources, i.e. either a game-specific classifier or real mixing in core audio; the
  HLE mix makes it impossible at the sink. Declined for now.
- Seats 2-4 still run the DSP and mix into the null sink, so the CPU saving of "no audio for
  hidden players" is not taken; only the device output is avoided.
- No per-seat volume control: the policy is all-or-nothing per console.
